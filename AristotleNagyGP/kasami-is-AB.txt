What the theorem says

The Lean theorem kasami is ab proves that the Kasami power function

• |F | = 2n ,
• n ≥ 1 and n is odd,
• k ≥ 1,
• gcd(k, n) = 1.
In the formal development, this is expressed as

Main proof steps

The Lean proof follows a short reduction chain:
1. Reduce the AB goal to a general moment criterion ab from moments.
2. Supply the Parseval identity for the Walsh spectrum.
3. Supply the fourth-moment identity coming from APN.
4. Supply the divisibility of every Walsh coefficient by a power of 2.
5. Conclude that the Walsh spectrum has the AB shape, so the function is Almost Bent.
